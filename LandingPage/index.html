// ARCHITECTURE & FLOW

How It Works Under the Hood

Engineered for ultra-low latency: zero-cloud video streaming with sub-second voice cues.

01 / CAPTURE

WebRTC Streaming

H.264 video stream captured directly from browser webcam with STUN peer-to-peer connection.

02 / EXTRACT

MediaPipe Pose

Extracts 33 3D keypoints at 30+ FPS directly in Python without cloud roundtrip delays.

03 / COMPUTE

Angle Calculus

Computes 3D joint trigonometry, checks movement thresholds, and detects form flaws.

04 / REASON

Groq LLM Engine

Synthesizes concise, actionable 10-15 word coaching cues in under 300 milliseconds.

05 / SPEAK

Voice Audio (TTS)

Streams spoken feedback audio directly into your headphones while you exercise.
