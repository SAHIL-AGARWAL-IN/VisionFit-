The coach

A coach that talks back.

When a form check fails, VisionFit sends the issue to a language model on Groq, which writes a short, specific cue. It's turned into speech and played in your browser. Cues are spaced out, and the same correction isn't repeated straight away, so you hear coaching rather than commentary.

  • Shallow squat“Sit deeper. Get your hips below your knees on this next one.”
  • Hips sagging“Hips are dropping. Squeeze your glutes and hold a straight line.”
  • Set complete“Set done. Shake it out and get ready for the next one.”

FAQ

Fair questions.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in a browser tab. You need a webcam and a name to sign in with. There's no password.

Is my video recorded?

No. Frames are streamed to the app server, analysed in memory and thrown away. Only your name and your set totals (exercise, reps, sets, time) are stored.

How accurate is it?

It measures 2D joint angles from a single camera, so camera position matters a lot. Side-on with your whole body in view works best. It's a training aid, not a medical or physio tool.

Why is the first load slow?

The app is hosted on Streamlit Community Cloud, which sleeps when nobody is using it. The first visit can take around 30 seconds to wake up.

What does it cost?

Nothing. VisionFit is a free personal project, and its source code is public on GitHub.
